# Scope and roadmap

## Current scope

- Public program overview, learning paths, and approved demo links.
- Guidance that combines technical practice and English communication.

## Out of scope

- Learner records, private feedback, credentials, client material, or funding administration.
- Replacing team-repository code, documentation, or ownership.
- Unapproved schedules, certificates, or access commitments.

Approve learning paths and demo-selection criteria first; add team links only after owner confirmation, content-safety review, and readiness validation.
